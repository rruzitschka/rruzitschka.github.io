What is SendLog?

SendLog is an intuitive iOS app designed specifically for rock climbers to easily log, track, and analyze their climbing activities. Quickly record your achievements, access detailed statistics, and keep notes for each climb. And it's free.

App Features

Log Climbing Routes

Record detailed information for each climb including route name, area, date, grade, personal rating, send type, and notes.

Photo Attachments

Attach photos to your climbing routes to capture beta, celebrate achievements, or document memorable climbs.

Training Session Tracking

Track your climbing training sessions separately from route logs. Record duration, type, and monitor consistency over time.

Project Tracking

Manage climbing projects, track attempts, record high points, update status, and convert projects into completed sends.

Voice Notes

Record and transcribe climbing notes using multilingual speech recognition in English, German, French, and Spanish.

Enhanced Sharing

Generate visually appealing achievement cards with send type icons for easy sharing on social media.

Comprehensive Statistics

Grade distribution charts, calendar heatmap, top areas, unique vs total climb modes, and time-period filtering.

Multi-Pitch & Route Types

Distinguish between Sport, Multi-Pitch, and Boulder routes with dedicated tracking and filtering for each style.

Data Import & Export

Export logs as a ZIP/CSV archive and import with full backward compatibility, including repeat ascent data.

☁️ Cloud Sync

Optionally sync climbs, ascents, and photos across devices. App works fully offline by default. Sign in with Apple.

Access your logbook from any browser after enabling Cloud Sync.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Can I add photos to my climbing routes?

Yes. Attach photos to each climb — stored locally and synced to the cloud if enabled.

Q: Is my data secure?

By default all data is stored locally. Cloud Sync uses Firebase with Sign in with Apple — no password is created or stored.

Q: How does Cloud Sync work?

Enable it in Settings → Cloud Sync and sign in with Apple. Climbs, ascents and photos sync in the background. The app works fully offline at all times.

Q: Can I export my data?

Yes — export as a CSV/ZIP from Settings. Works with or without Cloud Sync enabled.

Q: What is the Central Route Database?

A shared community route collection. Search when logging a send to pre-fill details and link your log to the shared route record.

Q: Does SendLog require internet?

No. SendLog works fully offline. Internet is only used for optional Cloud Sync.

Q: Can I delete my cloud account?

Yes — Settings → Delete My Account removes all server data while preserving local history.

Q: Can I track projects separately?

Yes. The Projects tab manages active, completed, and abandoned projects independently from your send log.
