SendLog is an iOS app designed for rock climbers to log, track, and analyze climbing activities. Record achievements, access detailed statistics, and keep notes for each climb — free, and offline-first.

Features

Log Climbing Routes

Route name, area, date, grade, rating, send type, and notes.

Photo Attachments

Attach photos to climbs — stored locally, synced to cloud.

Training Tracking

Dedicated training log separate from route logs.

Project Tracking

Manage projects, log attempts, track high points, convert to sends.

Voice Notes

Speech-to-text in English, German, French, Spanish.

Sharing

Generate achievement cards for social media.

Statistics

Grade charts, heatmap, unique vs total climbs, time-period filtering.

Route Types

Sport, Multi-Pitch, and Boulder — tracked and filtered separately.

Data Import & Export

CSV/ZIP export and import with full backward compatibility.

☁️ Cloud Sync

Optional cross-device sync via Firebase. Sign in with Apple. Offline by default.

🌐 Web App

Full logbook access from any browser.

Community routes with send/project counters and community star ratings.

Frequently Asked Questions

Yes. You can attach photos directly to each climb from your photo library or take a new one in the app. Photos are stored on-device and shown in the climb's detail view. If Cloud Sync is enabled, they are automatically uploaded and viewable in the Web App at sendlog.at/app.
By default, all your data is stored locally on your device and never leaves it. If you choose to enable Cloud Sync, your data is stored in Firebase (Google Cloud) and protected by Sign in with Apple — no password is ever created or stored.
Cloud Sync is entirely optional. Turn it on in Settings → Cloud Sync and sign in with Apple. Your climbs, ascents, and photos are then backed up and accessible across all your devices. The app continues to work fully offline at all times — Cloud Sync only runs when a connection is available.
Yes. From Settings you can export all your climbs and ascents as a CSV file inside a ZIP archive. This works whether or not Cloud Sync is enabled and gives you a portable backup you can open in any spreadsheet app. You can also export directly from the Web App.
The Central Route Database is a shared community collection of climbing routes. When logging a send or project, tap the 🔍 icon next to the Route Name field to search for the route. Selecting it pre-fills the form automatically and links your log to the shared record — so you can see how many other SendLog users have sent or projected the same route, and the community star rating.
No. SendLog works entirely offline — all your climbing logs and statistics are always available, even at the crag. An internet connection is only needed if you choose to enable the optional Cloud Sync feature.
